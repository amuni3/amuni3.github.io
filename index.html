Hi! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Montreal and Mila, where I am advised by Pierre-Luc Bacon. I also collaborate with Erick Delage at HEC Montreal. Previously, I received an MSc. in Robotics, Systems and Control from ETH Zurich and a BSc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Georgia Institute of Technology.

My research interests are primarily concerned with reinforcement learning, and particularly with methods of modeling uncertainty to improve safety and human-AI interaction.
